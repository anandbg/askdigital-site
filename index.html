How it works

  1. Ideate. We turn your problem into a short brief and a first screen sketch.
  2. Design. Agents draft the screens and the data. We cut it down to what you will use.
  3. Build. Agents write the code and the tests. We read the result before you do.
  4. Ship. To the web, the App Store or Google Play, under your name or ours.
  5. Maintain. Updates, fixes and costs stay with one team, so the app does not rot.
